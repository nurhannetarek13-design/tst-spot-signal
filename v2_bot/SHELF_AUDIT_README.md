# Existing strategy shelf: frozen audit protocol

The existing `spot_grid`, `fixed_dca` and `trend_breakout` trading rules are copied **without parameter changes** from `pionex_style.py` at source commit `317ebf74b3da86e440e06afae5b222e3b1dc98d4`.

Run `python -m v2_bot.shelf_profit_audit --output shelf_profit_audit.json` using Binance public endpoints. The report is an exploratory diagnostic only, not permission to trade. Input: 2025-09-01 (inclusive) to 2026-09-17 (exclusive); BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT; continuously verified 15m bars and fully closed 1h resampling. Split Q4 2025 / Jan-Apr 2026 holdout / May-Sep 2026 holdout. $50 per independent symbol/strategy/period alternative account; no compounding or pooling and no hidden optimization. Each decision uses fully closed bars, hypothetical fill at next 15m opening proxy, assumed 3bps full spread, 0.1% trading fee and 5bps slippage each side. Historic level-one bid/ask is unavailable. Every fill assumption is diagnostic and can differ from exchange execution.

Track realized cash versus unrealized mark, estimated end-liquidation costs, total equity drawdown, trade count, gross losses versus gains, unresolved inventory, and intrabar stop threshold touches missed by the original closed-bar strategy. Do not treat a favorable mark as booked profits, pretend stops were filled, or cherry-pick only a positive coin/period. A retrospective gate needs at least 30 closed trades, positive net after fees, PF above 1.2, no unverified intrabar stop touches, drawdown below $2 per scenario and no unresolved position/halt across **all three** coins and **both** holdout periods. Such a gate is a diagnostic threshold and by itself cannot authorize live execution.

Critical architectural limitation: `pionex_style.step` uses *observed subsequent book* stop/regime exit checks rather than attaching protective exchange-native stop orders. Historic OHLC may touch a trigger and rebound before the next observation. A $2 observed drawdown gate is not a guaranteed maximal loss; live routing stays OFF. Historical exchange minimum-notional and lot filters use **current** metadata as a proxy. Grid and fixed DCA may acquire more than one lot, but no leverage or martingale. Any future trading system must separately implement/reconcile stop/OCO placement, exchange filters, fill persistence, risk kill switch and audit evidence with user authorization. No source or paper ledgers are modified by this research audit.
